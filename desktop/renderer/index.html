Configuration Safety & Backup

Automatic multi-layer protection guarantees your settings, custom colors, companion style, and jar physics are never lost during updates.

100% Protected
How Your Configuration Is Protected:
  • Zero-Overwrite Packages: Official update archives do not contain settings files and cannot overwrite your active configurations.
  • Pre-Update Historical Snapshots: A timestamped snapshot is saved to your local backups folder prior to every update.
  • Live Dual-Mirror Backup: Every time you adjust colors, companion styles, or settings, a mirror backup is immediately synced.
  • Self-Healing Recovery: If settings.json is ever corrupted or deleted, Shing Live auto-recovers from the nearest snapshot on boot.
